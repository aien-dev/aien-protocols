# Protocol Governance

The AIEN Protocol Working Group develops open, implementation-neutral technical specifications and ABIs connecting autonomous agent semantics to accelerated inference hardware.

## Specification Development Process

1. **Working Drafts**: Specifications under active development reside in `specs/<domain>/SPEC.md` marked Draft.
2. **Candidate Approvals**: Candidate specifications undergo automated conformance testing across at least two independent runtimes.
3. **Approved Specifications**: Approved specifications are tagged, frozen with canonical cryptographic digests, and published.
4. **Contributions**: Inbound contributions are licensed under the Community Specification License 1.0 (for specifications) and Apache-2.0 (for code, schemas, and crates) following the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO).
